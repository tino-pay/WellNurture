## Task 1 — Sector Selection

**Innovation Sector:** Health & Wellness Innovation

**Project Title:** WellNurture: A Community Health and Wellness Guide

**Local Challenge:**

Students and community members may find it difficult to maintain healthy daily habits because of their busy schedules and limited access to simple, organized wellness information. Some may forget to drink enough water, take breaks, stay physically active, or maintain a regular sleep routine.

**Target Users:**

Students, young adults, and community members who want to improve their daily wellness habits.

**Proposed Solution:**

WellNurture is a responsive, single-page website that provides general health and wellness information and allows users to track simple daily habits. It aims to encourage users to become more aware of their routines through an easy-to-use interface.

**Key Features:**

1. Daily Wellness Checklist — Allows users to check or uncheck healthy habits they have completed.
2. Wellness Tips — Displays general educational tips about hydration, physical activity, rest, and everyday stress management.
3. Daily Progress Tracker — Shows the number and percentage of completed habits.

**Technology to Be Used:**

HTML for webpage structure, CSS for design and responsiveness, and vanilla JavaScript for interactivity.

**Project Scope:**

The project is limited to one responsive webpage with three core features. It will use demonstration content and will not require a backend, database, user authentication, or external APIs.

## Task 6 — Final Reflection

Through this activity, I learned that the way a prompt is written can affect the output generated by AI tools. The zero-shot prompt helped me begin the WellNurture project, but it provided only basic instructions. The structured prompt gave Copilot more context, technical constraints, and clear acceptance criteria.

Few-shot prompting helped me specify the format of the wellness checklist items by providing examples. Role assignment allowed me to ask Copilot to examine the project from an accessibility perspective. Structured analysis helped me review the code, identify a possible weakness, and plan how to verify a correction.

I also learned that generating code is not enough. I still need to inspect the source files, test the interactive features, and check whether the webpage works on different screen sizes. For the final version, I refined the layout, typography, and placement of content so WellNurture would have a different visual identity from AniSmart while preserving its main features.

Overall, this activity helped me understand the importance of clear prompts, responsible use of AI-generated code, and testing before accepting a final implementation.
