Notes for feeling well

A little knowledge,
lots of care.

Everyday ideas, not a perfect formula. Take what works for your life and leave the rest.

01 / HYDRATION

Keep water within reach.

Having a drink nearby can be a helpful reminder to hydrate throughout the day. Needs vary, so listen to your body and your care team's advice.

≈
02 / MOVEMENT

Move in a way that feels good.

A walk, a stretch, or an active trip across campus all count. Choose an option that suits your day and abilities.

↗
03 / REST

Make space to wind down.

A calming routine and a reasonably consistent sleep schedule may support restful sleep. Everyone's needs and schedules differ.

☾
04 / EVERYDAY STRESS

Take one thing at a time.

A brief pause, a few comfortable breaths, or talking with someone you trust can help you navigate a busy moment.

⌁

These tips are for general education, not diagnosis or treatment, and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.
